Trajectory analysis

Analysis turns motion into evidence.

PyMACS generates stability metrics, flexibility plots, compactness traces, contact maps, interaction summaries, ligand-centered network diagrams, and figurebooks.

RMSD

Tracks structural drift from a reference. A stable plateau is often reassuring; a continuing climb deserves inspection.

RMSF

Reports residue or atom flexibility. Peaks often indicate loops, termini, or contact regions that move substantially.

Rg

Measures compactness. It helps detect expansion, collapse, or large-scale breathing behavior.

Contacts

Identifies residues near the ligand or partner over time, separating persistent interactions from brief encounters.
